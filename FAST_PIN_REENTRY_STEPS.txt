Family DEFCON v5.8.3 Fast PIN Steps

1. Install this package.
2. Restart Home Assistant.
3. Go to Settings > Devices & services > Family DEFCON > Configure.
4. Open People, PINs, and AdGuard clients.
5. Re-enter each person's 4 digit PIN in the hidden new PIN field.
6. Save.
7. Restart Home Assistant or run family_defcon.reload_config.

Old hashes remain slow until the PINs are re-entered.
